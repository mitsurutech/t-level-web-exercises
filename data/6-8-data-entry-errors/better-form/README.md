# Better form: HTML, CSS and JavaScript exercise for T Level Digital Software Development

> **From the lesson:** [6.8: Data entry errors and how to stop them](https://mitsurutech.co.uk/resources/t-level/dsd-6-8-data-entry-errors)

## What to do

Make the trip form better. 1. Change the Year group box into a drop-down menu with Year 12 and Year 13. 2. Pre-fill the Trip date box with 2026-11-20. 3. Add a line of text under the second email box that says why we ask for it twice. Extra, if you want more: 4. In the JavaScript, check that the two emails match and show a message. Your teacher will look at the preview.

## Starter files

- `index.html`
- `style.css`
- `script.js`

Open `index.html` in a browser. Change `index.html`, `style.css` and `script.js` and reload the page to see the result.
